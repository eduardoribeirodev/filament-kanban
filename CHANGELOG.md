## v1.0.0 - 2026-10-16

Initial public release.

- Build Kanban boards from Eloquent queries or model collections.
- Use native Filament filters, infolists, actions, action groups, and shared forms.
- Drag whole cards or use optional handles, with persistent ordering and optional top insertion.
- Choose Default, Compact, Flat, or Accent layouts, or provide a custom card view.
- Compact card actions into a header menu or display them in the footer.
- Load cards with independent column pagination or progressive scrolling.
- Customize empty states, full column counts, and sums.
- Use compiled styles, light and dark themes, and English and Brazilian Portuguese labels.
- Explore the standalone documentation demo with fictional data and screenshots.
